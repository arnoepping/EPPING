# Roof v2 (branch `realism-bake`)

Baseline: the roof that is live on eppingmusic.com (`main`). Everything the `realism` branch did to the roof is dropped
(blue-hour sky, skyline boxes, glass railing, bar, crowd, DJ figure, smoke changes, 12 s autoplay, bar→DJ camera pan).
Changes below are improvements on the live roof only.

## Keep as live
- Sky gradient (PAL.bg → pink → orange), sun, Amsterdam skyline silhouettes with lit windows: unchanged.
- Festoon lights, smoke from the booth, camera walk onto the roof (9 s autoplay), finale video.

## Improve
1. **DJ booth** (refs: `media/inbox/DJ booth inspiration.webp`, `tomodachi club ceiling.jpeg`): a solid booth instead of
   the plank on legs. Front and sides in a diamond/scale relief, lit from under an overhanging dark top by a warm LED strip
   (grazing light, like the photos). Decks + mixer on top, a couple of small warm lights.
2. **Speakers**: one on each side of the booth, after the LD Systems Maui 28 G3 column PA (black sub at the bottom,
   slim column with a stack of small drivers on top, ~2 m tall).
3. **Terrace edge** (ref: `media/inbox/check the brick edge of the wall of the terrace for this photo.avif`): a clear
   low parapet around the terrace instead of the black slab: white/plastered inner face, red-orange rounded pantiles on
   top, brick outside. Stays dark (night), but readable.
4. **Floor**: wooden deck planks like the same photo, darker wood.

## How
Blender bake (like the street and stairwell): deck, parapet, booth and speakers baked with the light of the festoon
bulbs, the booth LED strip and the dusk sky; three.js keeps the skyline, sun, festoons, smoke and the pulse.
