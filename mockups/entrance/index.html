Five ways into the party

Every concept tells the same story as you scroll: you wait in line, get inside, and land on the dancefloor, where your footage takes over. Each one opens in Sunset rave or Gold rush, and you can switch palettes inside it. Tap Sound off to turn the sound on: murmur in the queue, muffled bass inside, the full track on the floor.

These are quick mockups to pick a direction. The floor buttons at the end don't go anywhere yet. The footage is the street-party clip. Concepts can also be combined, for example The Queue outside with On the List at the door.
